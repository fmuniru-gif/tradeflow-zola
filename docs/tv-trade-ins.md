# Controlled television trade-ins — 20261107-tv-trade-in-r1

Owner policy: accept only working televisions originally purchased from Zola; the customer upgrades to a larger new television. On acceptance the physical TV becomes a distinct used-stock product, quantity one, ready for normal resale after the acquisition upload is acknowledged. No trade-in data is created by installing the release.

Open **Sales Records → TV Trade-ins** as a signed-in Owner or Admin on an active managed device. Search the actual historical receipt or identified Quick Sale. Select the sale line and individual unit, record the physical serial, how the original purchase was established, functional inspection checks and cosmetic condition. Historical warranty serial evidence, where recorded for the original sale line, must match. Missing historical serial evidence is labelled a manual physical purchase check; software cannot certify an unrecorded serial. An original sale without an identifiable Customer Master name/telephone cannot be used.

Record both screen sizes, the new in-stock TV, agreed pre-VAT price, approved VAT/withholding percentages, accepted trade-in value, used-TV resale price and actual additional payment/wallet. Preview is read-only. Confirm valuation, customer agreement and actual payment before completing. The initial workflow requires full settlement: additional payment equals native net amount collectible minus trade-in value; no cash payout, debtor balance or overpayment/change is created. Original sale balances are retained; accepting a TV does not settle an earlier outstanding debt.

## Posting and synchronization

- Existing native tax calculation and stock allocation rules are reused. One normal protected `saveDB()` posts the sale, receipt, actual cash movement, used product/stock lot, acquisition/inspection/approval evidence, allocation audit and eligible warranty records. No new Cloud collection or server function is introduced.
- The trade-in value is the acquisition cost of the accepted TV, not cash inflow and not a discount on the new TV's price. Example: new TV net collectible GH₵3,000, credit GH₵500, payment GH₵2,500. Cash increases by GH₵2,500; used inventory increases by one TV at GH₵500 cost. The receipt records total settlement GH₵3,000 and separately identifies credit and cash.
- Every physical unit from the original sale has a deterministic SHA-256-derived operation ID, shared across devices. All linked patches use that one operation, native protocol sealing and durable exact-payload acknowledgement. Different concurrent acceptance payloads cannot silently be acknowledged as the same transaction. Native stock/cash/identity rejection rollback also handles a different payload for this specific trade-in operation; it does not weaken general mismatch handling.
- Approval requires online, initialized, active, ready Cloud state with no pending/failed operations, old-client detection or integrity warning. Background clock/Sync renders preserve the inspection form. Changing staff resets the draft. A fresh receive precedes acceptance. Changed source sale, stock, product, period, staff or Cloud context invalidates the preview. No Live Sync preference is changed.
- A network problem after the local save leaves one pending posting. The UI instructs the operator to await acknowledgement and not repeat it. The native FIFO entry point blocks resale of this acquisition while its operation is pending/failed. An exception after durable database persistence must not restore an older in-memory snapshot or cause another acceptance.
- No live business transactions, synthetic stock, physical inspection, device enrollment, recovery, schema migration or Cloud policy change is performed by release testing.

## Audit and limits

The acquisition is a native `inventoryTxns` STOCK_IN entry with subtype TV_TRADE_IN. Its details retain original sale/line/unit, customer, serial identity, inspection/verification, prices, approver, date, new receipt, used-stock IDs and operation ID. The used product and stock lot carry USED condition and source linkage. Operational and integrity fingerprints already cover these existing collections.

Native receipt preview/system print/direct print/PDF include credit and actual additional payment. Existing customer receipt references are attached through the established service. Ordinary receipt edits and independent inventory/cash undo cannot alter a linked trade-in or its original purchase. Completed trade-ins are immutable in this release; a whole-transaction reversal/cancellation requires a separately reviewed correction. The same original unit or serial cannot be accepted a second time, including a previously recorded/reversed acquisition. A future repeat-trade/reversal feature must model the full custody chain explicitly.

Tests use synthetic customers, staff, receipts, wallets and stock only. Source tests reuse assembled native tax/FIFO functions and inspect the actual operation builder. Browser tests use the shipped app, real local staff login, database/outbox persistence and receipt/PDF builders. Readiness/network calls are simulated and clearly isolated; passing tests do not claim a new physical trade-in was exercised on Main, Shop or Camon or that a real Cloud write was performed.

Delivery remains passive and preserves all site data/device IDs/queues. Update each existing app profile through `TV_TRADE_IN_UPDATE.html`, close all TradeFlow tabs/windows when instructed, and reopen. The release-specific delivery ID changes; the existing app-version identity is retained.
