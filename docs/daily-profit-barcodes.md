# Daily gross profit and barcode entry — 20261108-daily-profit-barcodes-r1

Daily Gross Profit is a small Dashboard tile alongside daily sales and quantity. It is read-only, calculated from active canonical sales/identified Quick Sales and recorded sale-time costs/FIFO evidence. Receipt register copies are not counted again. Revenue is after discounts, before VAT and expenses; withholding/payment timing is not profit. Reversed/void transactions do not count. Incomplete/missing cost or an unmatched active receipt produces Unavailable, never an assumed catalogue margin. The local calendar day follows the existing Daily Sales KPI and resets at midnight. Historical/closed Dashboard selections do not fabricate a live-day amount. No duplicate daily aggregate is persisted.

A compact Barcode / Product ID field appears in Stock In, Sale Out, Invoice, Quotation, Proforma, Waybill, Purchase Order, Stock Correction and the TV-upgrade product selector. Focus it (F8 selects the active visible entry), scan with a keyboard-mode USB/Bluetooth scanner, then Enter; manual typing/paste also works. Product IDs work immediately. Owner/Admin can link manufacturer barcodes to Product Catalog records in Products → Product barcodes. Leading zeros and barcode case are preserved. Unknown/ambiguous codes fail closed; concurrent conflicting mappings must be corrected by Owner/Admin. Barcode lookup is product selection only: it never adds quantity, changes prices or posts a transaction. All normal eligibility, price/PIN, quantity, role and Save/Preview rules remain authoritative. A model barcode does not establish an individual television's original purchase/serial identity.

Camera opens only after an explicit click and uses the rear camera preference, then native BarcodeDetector where available; a pinned local ZXing browser decoder provides fallback. Decode stops after a result. Camera tracks are stopped on close, hidden page, logout, navigation, changed staff/device context and late permission/decoder responses. Frames never leave the browser. Manual/handheld input remains available when camera permission or decoding is unavailable. Test coverage includes a real synthetic EAN13 video stream decoded by the actual bundled fallback, not just a mocked detector result.

## Fingerprint/device sign-in assessment

This release does not enable fingerprint login or change any authentication boundary. TradeFlow currently uses local shared-staff password verification separately from Cloud/device enrollment. A secure future passkey flow must register a credential against the correct active staff/device after reauthentication, issue unique short-lived account-bound challenges and verify the signed assertion, origin/RP ID and required user verification. Enrollment/removal, staff revocation, device replacement, credential loss and replay resistance need end-to-end coverage. It must retain password fallback and never bypass Owner MFA, PIN rules, device enrollment, recovery holds or five-minute inactivity logout.

Windows Hello and compatible phones may provide fingerprint, face or a device PIN; web applications cannot promise which method the device chooses. Raw fingerprints should never be collected or stored by TradeFlow. No sensor availability has been observed on the physical Main/Shop/Camon devices. Standards: https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/ ; scanner API/implementation: https://developer.chrome.com/docs/capabilities/shape-detection and https://github.com/zxing-js/browser .
